## Who We Are

CyaniAgent is an independent, open, and collaborative technology organization dedicated to bringing together software engineering, design aesthetics, and innovative ideas. We explore modern technologies and build open-source projects that aim to deliver meaningful, high-quality experiences across platforms and digital ecosystems.

Beyond software development, **CyaniAgent is also an open community** where people can exchange ideas, share knowledge, explore new possibilities, and collaborate on projects. We believe that great ideas can come from anywhere, and that an inclusive environment built on openness, mutual respect, and shared creativity can help turn those ideas into reality.

Whether you are a developer, designer, technology enthusiast, or simply someone curious about what we do, you are welcome to explore our work, participate in discussions, and contribute in ways that suit your interests and abilities.

## Our Principlaes

### Openness and Freedom

We value open-source software, accessible knowledge, and the freedom to explore different technologies and ideas. We strive to create an environment where people can learn, experiment, and express their creativity without unnecessary barriers.

### Collaboration and Community

We believe that technology grows stronger through collaboration. CyaniAgent encourages constructive discussions, knowledge sharing, and cooperation among contributors and community members. We welcome diverse perspectives and recognize that meaningful progress often comes from working together.

### Technology and Aesthetics

We see engineering and aesthetics as complementary rather than competing goals. Our projects seek to balance technical quality, performance, usability, and thoughtful design, creating software that is both capable and enjoyable to use.

### Exploration and Innovation

We are interested in exploring emerging technologies, challenging conventional approaches, and experimenting with new ideas. Not every experiment needs to become a finished product; the process of learning, testing, and sharing discoveries is valuable in its own right.

### Open Internet and Decentralization

We are interested in open protocols, decentralized systems, federated social networks, self-hosted services, and technologies that empower people to participate in a more open and interconnected digital world.

## What We Do

Our projects span several areas of software development and technological exploration, including:

- **Decentralized and Federated Social Platforms** — Exploring open social ecosystems, interoperability, and user-controlled services.
    
- **Protocols and Backend Infrastructure** — Designing and experimenting with technologies that support distributed systems and interconnected services.
    
- **Cross-Platform Applications** — Building software and development tools for modern platforms and diverse user environments.
    
- **Linux and Desktop Technologies** — Exploring desktop environments, user interfaces, and the integration of modern design with system-level technologies.
    
- **Web Development and Digital Experiences** — Creating websites, content platforms, and supporting tools that connect projects with their users and communities.
    

Our projects evolve over time. Some are actively developed, while others remain experimental or under exploration. We value the opportunities to learn, iterate, and improve throughout the development process.

## Our Vision

We envision a digital ecosystem where technology is not only powerful and efficient, but also open, expressive, and accessible to the people who use it.

Through open-source development, community collaboration, and continuous exploration, CyaniAgent seeks to connect aesthetics with underlying technology—and to make the process of creating, sharing, and discovering technology an experience everyone can be part of.
