From an external perspective or based on common perceptions of code hosting platforms (such as GitHub), **CyaniAgent** is often first recognized as a **developer organization** focused on open-source software, client-side development, and technological exploration.

However, this one-dimensional definition is incomplete. To avoid misunderstandings regarding our identity, we would like to clarify: **CyaniAgent is not merely an open, free, and collaborative technical organization; it is also a community of creators that is open to all, free, and encourages open discussion.**

---

## I. CyaniAgent as a “Technical Organization”: Open, Free, and Collaborative

In terms of engineering practices and project advancement, CyaniAgent functions as an efficient open-source technical collaboration hub:

* **Engineering Exploration Focused on Aesthetics and Performance**: We are deeply engaged in the research and development of the Fediverse (e.g., the ActivityPub/Misskey ecosystem), modern lightweight forum architectures (e.g., the Flarum ecosystem), as well as cross-platform clients and underlying toolchains (covering technology stacks such as Flutter/Dart, Rust, TypeScript, and Kotlin), pursuing the ultimate in design aesthetics and high-performance technical implementation.
* **Decentralized, Agile Collaboration**: We have no rigid hierarchical barriers. Whether it’s core applications like `CyaniTalk`, `Nyachi`, and `Asagity`, or various utility libraries and edge tools, we follow a modern open-source workflow characterized by high transparency, open contribution, and asynchronous collaboration.

---

## II. CyaniAgent as a “Creator Community”: Open Invitations and Free Expression

More essential than the code repositories are the people and culture gathered here. CyaniAgent is, at its core, a vibrant **community of shared interests and creativity**:

1. **Open Invitation**
   * Our doors are always open. You don’t need extensive development experience to join—whether you’re a visual creator skilled in UI design, a translation contributor passionate about multilingual localization, a user experience tester dedicated to testing and providing feedback, or simply a fellow enthusiast curious about the federated universe and geek culture, CyaniAgent extends a sincere invitation to you.
2. **Freedom to Explore**
   * Here, inspiration knows no bounds. The community encourages members to turn their wildest ideas into reality—from social apps on smart wearables to anime-style interface themes, to experimental local players or database tools, any interesting idea can find resonance and fertile ground here.
3. **Speak Freely**
   * Compared to cold issue lists and code reviews, we equally value the warmth and spark found in everyday conversations. In our discussion channels, in-depth debates on technical architecture, creative exchanges on design aesthetics, daily sharing of ACG culture, and visions for the future of the internet can all unfold without restraint. Equality, friendliness, and respect for every voice form the foundation of our community communication.

---

## III. Summary: The Organization Is the Skeleton; the Community Is the Soul

| Dimension | Traditional Technical Organization Perspective | CyaniAgent’s Comprehensive Positioning |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Core Structure** | Collection of code repositories, project maintenance teams | **Technical Collaboration Organization + Open-Culture Community** |
| **Barriers to Entry** | Focus on code commits and engineering skills | **Open invitations**; diverse roles such as design, translation, testing, and creativity are welcome |
| **Collaboration Style** | Task-driven, modular division of labor | **Free, autonomous, and boundary-less collaboration** |
| **Communication Atmosphere** | Focused on specific development topics | **Open and candid discussion**, balancing hardcore geek spirit with relaxed exchanges among like-minded individuals |

**In short:**
**“The organization”** is the collaborative framework through which CyaniAgent delivers high-quality open-source projects, while **“the community”** is the soul that enables CyaniAgent to continue growing. We come together because of our shared passion, collaborate in an open and free environment, and create together through open dialogue.
